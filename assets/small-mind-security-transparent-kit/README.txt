SMALL MIND SECURITY — BRAND KIT

Based on the supplied Cyborg Tactical Profile with Purple Node.png.
Transparent master made with the built-in image generation editor.
The exterior background is transparent; internal dark details are opaque.
This is an AI-assisted extraction, not a pixel-identical copy of the source.

FILES
logo-transparent.png: transparent master artwork (1254 x 1254).
icon-N.png: PNG exports at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 180, 192,
256, 384, 512, and 1024 pixels.
favicon.ico: multi-resolution ICO (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256).
favicon.svg and logo-faithful.svg: exact artwork embedded in an SVG.
These two SVG files contain raster artwork, not editable vector paths.
logo-vector.svg: editable color-traced vector paths. This approximates
the original texture and shading; use logo-faithful.svg for exact appearance.
apple-touch-icon.png: 180 pixels.
android-chrome-192x192.png and android-chrome-512x512.png: app icons.
site.webmanifest: basic web app metadata and icon references.
html-snippet.html: favicon tags to paste into your site's <head>.
preview.html: open locally to inspect the supplied artwork and SVG versions.

INSTALLATION
Copy the kit contents into your site's public root and paste the snippet
into <head>. For a subfolder deployment, update snippet paths and start_url.
The manifest alone does not add offline support or full PWA functionality.

NOTES
All included PNG, ICO, and SVG assets have transparent backgrounds.
Fine helmet details become less distinct at tiny favicon sizes.
Accent metadata color: #a22de5.
The manifest does not force a background color.

EDIT PROMPT
Use case: background-extraction. Remove ONLY the black exterior background,
replacing it with true transparent alpha. Preserve the complete right-facing
tactical cyborg head, dark charcoal helmet and face, black internal outlines
and seams, headset, microphone, straps, purple shield and circular node.
Preserve geometry, composition, margins, colors and shading. Do not redesign
or brighten the logo. Preserve enclosed dark areas within the artwork as
opaque; remove surrounding background and genuine exterior gaps. Clean
antialiased edges; no white fill or checkerboard baked in.
