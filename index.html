What we hold to

01

Provenance or nothing

Every conclusion carries where it came from and how well it's supported. A claim that can't show its lineage isn't admissible.

02

Refusal is a feature

When the evidence isn't there, the honest output is to decline, not to guess fluently. A system that can't say "I don't know" will confidently tell you anything.

03

Confidence is survival

A belief is worth holding once it has survived a real attempt to knock it down. Everything gets tested, and the failures are kept.

04

The record is public

The work ships with its receipts: fingerprinted artifacts, an append-only ledger, and preserved failures that were never quietly relabeled.
