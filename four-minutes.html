Open dataset, in progress

Four minutes of you thinking out loud.

I need recordings of ordinary unedited thought, and there is almost no such thing on the internet.

What I am asking for

Record yourself for three to five minutes on your phone, talking out loud while you are working on something or stuck on something. Dishes, code, a seized bolt in the garage, a form you have been putting off. Not a description of your inner voice, not an explanation of anything. Just talk while you do a thing.

The first minute is always self-conscious. That is expected, and I throw it away.

What it should sound like

Sample, typed out instead of recorded
i wonder if people would do this if i asked i bet they would if i got it in front of the right people but i have so much to do still i need to fnish my application for wgu and start the sophia learning what are the boys fighting about now i bet the youngest didnt even finish his food oh great now its quiet up there im sure hell start screaming again in a second oh i still have to pay for the sophia subscription before i can even start and thats a hundred bucks yep hes screaming again

Boring is the point. Nothing in there resolves, nothing gets explained, and it changes subject four times in six lines. If yours sounds interesting you are performing for the microphone and I cannot use it.

How to do it

01

Use the recorder already on your phone

Nothing to install, no account, no app.

02

Start it, put it down, carry on

Go back to whatever you were doing before you opened this page.

03

Talk

Trail off, repeat yourself, lose the thread, contradict yourself. All of that is the useful part.

04

Stop around four minutes

Do not wrap up and do not conclude. Stopping mid-thought is correct.

If you do a few across a week they get better, because by the third one you have stopped noticing the recorder. Whatever format your phone records in is fine. The one thing that breaks the upload is an iPhone set to Lossless under Settings, Voice Memos, Audio Quality, which makes four minutes too large to send.

What happens to your recording

  • LOCAL
    Transcribed on my own machine. It is not sent to any transcription service.
  • DELETED
    The audio is destroyed once it is transcribed. Your voice is never kept and never published.
  • SCRUBBED
    Names, employers, addresses and anything else identifying are stripped from the text.
  • PUBLISHED
    Only the processed text is released. The whole point is a dataset that can actually be shared, and that means the raw material cannot be.
  • YOUR CALL
    Do not record anything you would mind existing in text form. Four careful minutes beats something you regret sending.

Why this does not already exist

I am training a small model that runs in a loop continuously, reading from a memory store, thinking, and passing things up to a larger model when it finds something worth passing. It has to learn from what unstructured thought actually looks like rather than from finished writing.

So I went looking for a corpus. What you can download today is 45,000 transcripts of people spending fifteen seconds choosing between two gambles, one ninety-one second audio clip, and ten hours of a developer in Bratislava debugging a Xamarin app in Slovak. That is the public yield.

Everything else is locked up. Researchers recorded thousands of hours of this over forty years, published the analysis, and kept the recordings, because a voice is identifying data and the consent forms only ever covered the original study. The method is old and the material is gone.

Send a recording

No account needed and no email required. Nobody is getting paid, me included. If it works the corpus gets published, with credit to anyone who wants it.
